What he does all day

He fishes. You do the work.

A useless uncle with expenses

He walks in, forgets his beer and begs you for $5. Click “Give him $5” for a pretend payment. He jogs off, comes back with a can and settles into his camping chair on your Dock.

He fishes, you do the work

Random bites bring a notification and a screen shake. Hold the mouse on him to reel before the fish escapes. Land one for your fish book and menu bar count, then watch him dance to banjo as if he helped.

Bad air runs in the family

He watches your screen time and scolds you for skipping breaks. He dozes, snores and scolds you again if you wake him or click him. Then he farts in his chair and blames you, in a voice like a sailor gargling gravel.

You can send him home

Hover over him or right-click for his controls. Mute him, close his bubble or send him home. Family visits need an end time.

Install on your Mac

Two minutes. One grumpy roommate.

This version isn't notarized by Apple, so macOS may block the first launch. The steps approve this app individually.

Download for Mac
  1. Open the downloaded Fish-All-Day.dmg and drag Fish All Day into Applications.
  2. Open Fish All Day from Applications to see Apple could not verify “Fish All Day” is free of malware. Click Done, not Move to Trash.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and scroll down to Security. Find “Fish All Day” was blocked to protect your Mac, click Open Anyway, then confirm Open Anyway with your password or Touch ID.
  4. Uncle Farter walks onto your Dock. You only do this once; after that, the app opens normally.

Questions

Before you adopt him

Is it free?

Yes. The app is free. His “Give him $5” button is part of the joke and doesn't charge you. Your uncle has a rich life on fake money.

Will it slow down my Mac?

Drawing him in 3D uses some processing power, memory and battery. The impact depends on your Mac and what else is running. Send him home to pause his activity and drawing. Even doing nothing takes effort.

Can I mute him?

Yes. Hover over him or right-click to mute his voice or all sound. Close a speech bubble to stop that line. Send him home when you've had enough of the burps, nonsense and bleeped swears.

Is it safe?

He may throw a stone when he wants you to take a break. The screen crack is a visual effect, and the beer button takes no real money. This release isn't notarized, so Apple hasn't checked it through that process.

Which macOS does it need?

The current download is for macOS 13 Ventura or later on an Apple silicon Mac, M1 or newer. It doesn't support Intel Macs. He's picky for a man who brought his own chair.
