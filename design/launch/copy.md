Captions appear on frame one. Each caption below has six words or fewer; separate lines appear in sequence unless marked as an end card. Keep captions inside a 5% margin, clear of him and the Dock. Hold text still while it is readable. Dialogue is quoted verbatim from `web/src/lines.js`; the key beside each quote identifies its group. Use his voice in the edit, with music lowered beneath it. If a recorded speech bubble is visible, it must match the chosen line. Bleep `#@$%!` rather than speaking the symbols. No narrator.

### Four features

**Your Dock is his retirement plan**

He walks in, forgets his beer and asks you for $5. Click to fund his pretend beer run. He returns, opens a can and settles into his chair. Your desktop shows through around him.

**He fishes. You do the reeling.**

Bites arrive at random with a notification and a shake. Hold the mouse on him to reel before the fish escapes. Land it for your fish book and menu bar count. He celebrates with a banjo dance.

**An old man with office hours**

He watches how long you've been at your Mac and tells you to take a break. Between naps, snores and bleeped complaints, he might throw a stone at your screen. The crack is fake. The tantrum is his.

**You can send him home**

Hover over him or right-click for his controls. Mute his voice, close his speech bubble or send him home. “Somehow, your silence has an attitude.”

### Install on your Mac

1. Download Fish All Day and open the disk image.
2. Drag Fish All Day into Applications.
3. In Applications, right-click Fish All Day, choose **Open**, then confirm **Open** if offered.
4. If macOS still blocks it, open **System Settings > Privacy & Security**. Scroll to the message about Fish All Day and choose **Open Anyway**. Authenticate if asked, then confirm **Open**.

This version isn't notarized by Apple, so macOS may block the first launch. The steps above approve this app individually.

### FAQ

**Is it free?**

Yes. The app is free. His “Give him $5” button is part of the joke and doesn't charge you. He's terrible with imaginary money.

**Will it slow down my Mac?**

Drawing him in 3D uses some processing power, memory and battery. The impact depends on your Mac and what else is running. Send him home to pause his activity and drawing.

**Can I mute him?**

Yes. Hover over him or right-click to mute his voice or all sound. Close a speech bubble to stop that line. Send him home when you've had enough of him.

**Is it safe?**

His screen crack is a visual effect, and the beer button takes no real money. This release isn't notarized, so Apple hasn't checked it through that process.

**Which macOS does it need?**

The current download is for macOS 13 Ventura or later on an Apple silicon Mac, M1 or newer. It doesn't support Intel Macs.
