Hey folks! My #Manycorehackathon entry: **Fish All Day**, a free Mac app. **Uncle Farter** is a grumpy retired 3D cartoon uncle living on your Dock. He fishes all day, begs for $5 pretend beer money, farts and blames you, naps, makes you reel, gets dragged underwater by big fish and throws a stone at your screen for overworking.

*The most useless uncle in your Mac family. Wild manners. Proudly useless.*

His look: Aholo’s image tool. Model, rig, animations, chair, cooler, beer can and fish: Lux3D.
