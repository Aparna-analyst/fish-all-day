# Fish All Day: launch trailer scripts

Both versions run **50 seconds**, in **16:9**, for the hackathon submission and the X post.

## Shared edit notes

- Use real recordings of the Mac app, with a code editor open and his strip above the Dock. Reframe or jump cut existing footage; keep the Dock visible whenever it explains the action. Only the title cards and the four-second Aholo beat leave the desktop footage.
- Hold captions and title lettering pixel-still inside a 5% margin on every edge. Keep captions clear of his face, speech bubbles, the payment button and reel feedback. Each caption has at most seven words, including the seven-word end card.
- The required sentence, "The most useless uncle in your Mac family", runs across two consecutive cards: "The most useless uncle" then "in your Mac family". The other tagline appears intact: "Wild manners. Proudly useless."
- Voice entries identify either the narrator or the exact dialogue.json moment key. Use the existing Uncle recordings at their original speed, only at the matching moments. Keep other recorded dialogue out of these edits. Script A adds a deep, completely serious trailer narrator; Script B has no narrator or other new speech.
- The $5 is pretend money. The successful catch and the big-fish loss are separate encounters. The cracked screen is the actual app effect. Stop the big-fish shot at the splash; no pulledIn line is used because that key belongs to his return from the water.
- Each beat specifies one featured sound effect. Duck music for speech and respect the written silences. Use instrumental music throughout, with no sung words.
- The Aholo beat uses the supplied concept image, actual model, actual rig and an existing move. Keep "Modelled, rigged and animated with Aholo Lux3D" on screen for its full four seconds.
